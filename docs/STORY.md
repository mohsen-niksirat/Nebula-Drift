NEBULA DRIFT — Story

The nebula was once the galaxy's busiest trade corridor. Two
centuries ago an event called the Drift tore it apart: space itself
began folding, spitting out rogue drones, derelict fleets, and
things no one has a name for. Every ship that enters is lost.

You pilot the Kestrel-9, an experimental scout with a prototype
"Echo Drive" — the only engine that can hold a course through the
folding. Your mission: cross the Nebula's nine sectors and reach
the Beacon, a distress array still transmitting from the far side.

Death is not the end. The Echo Drive remembers: every run, a fragment
of your ship's pattern survives back at the last station. The station
engineers fuse those fragments into permanent hull refits and weapon
blueprints (meta-progression). The Nebula changes every time (procedural
sectors), and so do the salvage offers between sectors (upgrade picks).

Tone: lonely neon space, mystery — what is the Beacon? Why does the
Nebula resist you more the closer you get? Final-sector enemies
flicker like they're... remembering previous runs too.
